ClipSnatcher is ready

Three ways to save a video from X, Reddit or Facebook.

  1. 1

    Point and save

    Move the pointer over a video. A Save button appears in its top-right corner.

  2. 2

    Right-click

    Right-click a video or a link to a post and choose the ClipSnatcher item.

  3. 3

    Paste a link

    Click the ClipSnatcher icon in the toolbar (or press Alt+Shift+V), paste a post link and press Save.

Good to know

  • Reload tabs that were already open. The Save button only appears on X, Reddit and Facebook tabs opened or reloaded after installing.
  • Pin the icon. Click the puzzle piece in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to ClipSnatcher.
  • Videos land in Downloads/ClipSnatcher, sorted by site and named after the post. Everything you save is listed in History, where you can search by title.
  • If a feed video can't be matched to its post, ClipSnatcher asks you to open the post first rather than guess.
  • Nothing leaves your computer. No accounts, no tracking, no servers.
